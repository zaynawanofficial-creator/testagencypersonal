Who we work with

A good fit if you sell something people already search for.

We'd rather tell you now than six months in. Here is where our approach works well, and where it doesn't.

Usually a strong fit

  • UK service businesses that win work through search, such as trades, clinics, professional services and local retail.
  • Online stores with products people actively look for and margins that can support marketing.
  • Owners who can give us access, answer questions and approve content within a few days.

Probably not the right fit

  • You need guaranteed page-one rankings or a set number of leads. No honest agency can promise either.
  • You need results within weeks and have no budget for ads while organic work builds.
  • You're looking for bought followers, likes or reviews. We don't sell them. Depends on retiring engagement packages (B4)

How we work

Four steps, and you'll know what's happening at each.

Every engagement follows the same pattern, whether it's a one-off audit or ongoing SEO. Read our full approach.

  1. Diagnose

    We review your site, search visibility, competitors and current tracking before recommending anything.

    You get: a short findings summary

  2. Plan

    We agree priorities, scope, cost and the measures we'll report on. Nothing starts without your sign-off.

    You get: a written scope and quote

  3. Deliver

    We fix, write and build in order of likely impact, and share work for approval before it goes live.

    You get: a visible task list

  4. Report

    We report plainly on what changed, what moved and what we'll do next, including what didn't work.

    You get: a monthly report and call

Why trust us

We earn trust with how we work, not big promises.

Search results depend on competitors, algorithm changes and your market, none of which anyone controls. What we can control is how carefully we work and how openly we report. These are the commitments we put in writing.

Approve these four commitments, since each one must be honoured in practice

  • No guaranteed rankings

    We'll forecast carefully and explain our reasoning, but we won't promise positions, traffic or AI citations.

  • You own everything

    Your Google, ad and analytics accounts stay in your name. Leave any time and keep all of it.

  • Scope before spend

    A written scope and price before any work begins. Changes are agreed, never surprise invoices.

  • Plain-English reporting

    Measures agreed up front, reported monthly, with what we did and why.
