The problem we solve

Most small business websites don't fail at SEO because of one big mistake. They fail because of a stack of small ones: pages that target nothing in particular, service areas buried in a footer, slow templates, thin product descriptions, and no clear reason for Google, or a buyer, to prefer you over the next result.

Our job is to find which of those problems is actually holding you back, fix them in order of likely impact, and build the pages and signals that match how your customers search.

Who SEO services suit

A good fit

  • People already search for what you sell, such as "accountant in Leeds" or "linen bedding UK"
  • You can invest for at least six months, or pair SEO with ads meanwhile
  • You can approve content and give site access promptly

Not a good fit

  • A brand-new product nobody searches for yet. Paid social may suit you better
  • You need a guaranteed position or lead count
  • You can't make changes to your website

What's included in ongoing SEO

The exact mix depends on what the audit finds. A typical monthly scope covers: confirm scope

  • Technical SEO: crawlability, indexing, site speed, mobile issues, redirects, duplicate content and structured data that accurately describes your pages
  • Keyword and intent mapping: one clear purpose per page, so your pages stop competing with each other
  • On-page optimisation: titles, headings, internal links and copy improvements on priority pages
  • Content: new or rewritten service, location or category pages, briefed around real questions buyers ask
  • Local SEO (where relevant): Google Business Profile, consistent business details and location pages
  • Authority: legitimate mentions and links from relevant, real sites. No paid link networks
  • Measurement: Search Console and analytics set up correctly, with enquiry or sales tracking where possible

Deliverables

  • Audit findings and a prioritised action plan
  • A shared task list showing what's done, in progress and next
  • A monthly report: work completed, visibility and traffic changes, enquiries or sales where tracked, and next month's priorities

How an SEO engagement works

  1. Week 1

    Access and baseline

    We get read access to Search Console, analytics and your CMS, and record where you stand today so progress can be measured honestly.

  2. Weeks 1–3

    Audit and plan

    Technical crawl, content and competitor review, and keyword mapping. You receive a prioritised plan and we agree the first three months of work.

  3. Month 1 onwards

    Fix and build

    We work through the plan by impact: technical fixes first, then priority pages, then content and authority. Anything customer-facing is shared for approval.

  4. Monthly

    Report and adjust

    A written report and a short call. We keep what's working, drop what isn't, and agree next month's priorities.

How long SEO takes

Technical fixes can show up within weeks of being deployed and re-crawled. Changes in rankings and traffic from new or improved pages usually take several months, and depend on your competition, your site's history and how quickly changes go live.

We don't guarantee rankings, traffic, AI citations or sales. Anyone who does is either guessing or using tactics that put your site at risk. What we commit to is the work, the reasoning behind it and transparent reporting.

What we need from you

  • Access to Google Search Console, analytics and your website (we'll send a simple access guide)
  • A named contact who can answer questions about your services, customers and locations
  • Approval of content and changes within an agreed turnaround, typically five working days
  • Developer time if your site is managed by another agency and we can't make changes directly

What's not included

  • Paid advertising spend or management. Available separately through paid advertising
  • Full website redesigns or rebuilds. See website design & development
  • Bought links, private blog networks, fake reviews or any tactic that breaches search engine guidelines
  • Content volumes beyond the agreed monthly scope. Extra work is quoted before it starts
